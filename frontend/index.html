HOW PENNYWISE WORKS

One request.
Real-time results.

PennyWise queries India's national eRaktKosh blood inventory system directly, parses the stock data, and shows you matching results in seconds — not minutes.

  1. 01
    Tell us what to look for

    Blood group, component, units and the area to search.

  2. 02
    eRaktKosh is queried directly

    We hit the government portal's API in real-time. No browser automation delays.

  3. 03
    Results ranked & displayed

    Available stock is shown first, ranked by freshness and location relevance.

  4. 04
    Confirm directly

    Use the phone number or directions to contact the facility and confirm current stock.
